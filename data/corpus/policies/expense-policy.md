# Acme Expense Policy

## Caps

- Meal cap: **USD 75** per person per meal.
- Hotel cap: **USD 250** per night.
- Receipts required above **USD 25**.

## Foreign currency

Foreign exchange rates are not stored in Acme docs; use live rates for USD to INR.

Submit expenses within 30 days via Concur.
