# Acme PTO Policy

## Eligibility

Full-time employees accrue paid time off based on role family.

## Engineer accrual

- Engineers receive **25 days** of PTO per calendar year.
- Monthly accrual is **2.08 days**.
- Maximum carryover into the next year is **5 days**.
- Blackout months for engineering: November, December.

## Request process

Submit requests in Workday at least 5 business days in advance. Managers on `acme.internal` must approve.
